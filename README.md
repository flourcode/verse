# Better Verses

**betterverses.com** · When you care, but don’t know what to say.

There is a pause before you hit send.

A friend has lost her mother. Your father goes into surgery on Tuesday. A coworker was laid off. You want to say something from Scripture, and you stop, because you want it to be the right thing and not a line that sounds nice but was written about something else. Sometimes the pause ends in silence. Sometimes it ends with the first verse that came up.

I built this for that pause.

## Why

Scripture comforts people. It also gets used carelessly, and the people it lands on are usually at their lowest. Romans 8:28 read at a child's funeral sounds like the loss was for the best. Jeremiah 29:11 sent to someone laid off yesterday promises a prosperity it never promised; it was written to exiles who would not go home for seventy years. "God won't give you more than you can handle," said to someone drowning, tells them their collapse is a failure of capacity, and it is not even in the Bible.

None of the people who send those verses mean harm. They reach for something, and the thing they reach for is wrong for the moment. Every existing verse website makes that worse, because they are lists: forty verses about grief, pulled by keyword, with no note on who said each one, to whom, or why. A list is a search result. It is not a recommendation.

So this site does three things differently.

**It starts from the moment, not the book.** Losing a spouse. Waiting for test results. A child who has cut you off. A dog that died. Each page has seven verses I chose after reading the whole chapter around each one, with a note on why it fits, and on most pages the popular verse I would leave out and why. Not because it is a bad verse, but because it is the wrong one for this room.

**It searches the whole Bible in plain English.** All 31,000 verses, in your browser, with nothing sent anywhere. You can type "my friend just lost her mother," or "that verse about the birds," or "be still and know," or a saying you suspect is not in the Bible at all, and it will tell you. When a search sounds like a hard moment, it ranks the verses that comfort first and says so, with a switch to turn that off. Nothing is hidden. Some of what is in the Bible is not comforting, and I would rather show you that honestly than pretend.

**It says who is speaking.** Every verse on every page comes with who said it, to whom, and what was happening. That is the whole method, and it is something you can learn to do yourself in two minutes; there is a page that teaches it, and I would be pleased if it made the site unnecessary for you.

## Who it is for

Mostly, people who want to comfort someone they care about. A grandmother in the card aisle. A man typing and deleting a text to a friend in hospice. A daughter looking for something to read at her father's bedside. And pastors, chaplains, and Bible teachers, who live in that pause forty times a year and deserve tools that do not send them to a sermon marketplace.

And sometimes that someone is you.

## What I believe about it

Six things, in one sentence each, on the site at [/what-we-believe/](https://betterverses.com/what-we-believe/). The short version: read the paragraph, not the line; keep the promises the text actually makes; say why it fits or do not send it; fewer, better; no agenda; and sometimes the verse is not what they need. Presence matters more than words, and a crisis needs a doctor before it needs a passage.

## Who made it

One person, of quiet faith, who got tired of the pause ending badly. I am not a pastor or a scholar. I read a lot, I check everything against the whole chapter, and I write it down in plain words. The site is free, it stays free, nobody pays to have a verse or a link on it, and if that ever changes it will say so on the About page before it changes anywhere else.

If a verse here is out of place, or a note misreads a passage, or someone you know needs a page that is not here yet, write to hello@betterverses.com. I read everything.

## The Bible text

All Scripture is from the World English Bible, a modern-English translation in the public domain. It is not the translation most people carry to church; I use it because it can be reproduced in full, which lets the site show whole passages and lets you copy, print, and share any verse without a license. The search understands the wording you remember from the King James, the NIV, or wherever you grew up, and shows you the WEB. Every result gives the reference so you can read it in your own Bible.

## If you are here for the code

It is a plain static site: content in JSON, templates in JavaScript, no framework, no database, no API, no accounts. The full-Bible search runs entirely in the browser. Everything about building and deploying it is in [DEVELOPMENT.md](DEVELOPMENT.md). You are welcome to learn from it.
