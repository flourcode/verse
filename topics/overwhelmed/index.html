Bible verses for feeling overwhelmed

Feeling overwhelmed usually means the load is bigger than the person carrying it. Most of these verses accept that as the starting point. They're about weakness, rest, and what happens when you stop trying to hold everything up yourself.

A verse to start with

For when you're overwhelmed

Matthew 11:28-29

Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart; and you will find rest for your souls.

World English Bible (WEB)

Why this fits

Jesus invites the exhausted specifically: 'all you who labor and are heavily burdened.' He doesn't offer a lighter schedule; he offers rest and a different yoke, one carried alongside him. 'Gentle and humble in heart' is how he describes himself, which is a relief if the voice in your head driving you is neither.

7 verses for when you're overwhelmed

  1. Matthew 11:28-29

    Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart; and you will find rest for your souls.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Gospel (Jesus’ words and life)

    Jesus invites the exhausted specifically: 'all you who labor and are heavily burdened.' He doesn't offer a lighter schedule; he offers rest and a different yoke, one carried alongside him. 'Gentle and humble in heart' is how he describes himself, which is a relief if the voice in your head driving you is neither.

  2. Psalm 61:2

    From the end of the earth, I will call to you when my heart is overwhelmed. Lead me to the rock that is higher than I.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Psalm (poetry)

    'When my heart is overwhelmed' is the phrase, and it's in the Bible, from the end of the earth, from someone far from where he wants to be. He asks to be led to a rock higher than himself, a place to stand that he couldn't climb to alone. That's the request when you can't.

  3. 2 Corinthians 12:9

    He has said to me, “My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.” Most gladly therefore I will rather glory in my weaknesses, that the power of Christ may rest on me.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Letter (epistle)

    Paul asked three times for a hardship to be removed, and the answer was no, with this sentence attached: 'My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.' Being overwhelmed is weakness, and this verse says weakness is where the strength shows up. It's the opposite of the advice to try harder.

  4. Psalm 55:22

    Cast your burden on Yahweh and he will sustain you. He will never allow the righteous to be moved.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Psalm (poetry)

    Cast the burden, and 'he will sustain you.' The word for burden can mean what's been given to you, your lot. David wrote this during a betrayal. The promise is holding up under it, not that the lot changes overnight.

  5. Isaiah 40:29

    He gives power to the weak. He increases the strength of him who has no might.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Prophecy

    'He gives power to the weak.' Two verses later comes the famous 'wings like eagles' line, but it starts here, with an admission of having no might left. The chapter is addressed to people who think God has stopped noticing them. It's the right chapter for running on empty.

  6. Exodus 14:14

    Yahweh will fight for you, and you shall be still.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Narrative

    Trapped between an army and the sea, the people panic. Moses says God will fight, and 'you shall be still.' Sometimes the overwhelming thing is that you think every outcome depends on your effort. This verse describes a situation where the outcome didn't.

  7. Psalm 62:1-2

    My soul rests in God alone. My salvation is from him. He alone is my rock, my salvation, and my fortress. I will never be greatly shaken.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Psalm (poetry)

    'My soul rests in God alone.' The word 'alone' does a lot of work: not in God plus a plan, not in God once things calm down. The result is 'I will never be greatly shaken,' which is honest, since 'greatly' admits some shaking still happens.

How to use these verses

When you're overwhelmed, don't add 'read the Bible more' to the list. Read one verse. Matthew 11:28 is enough for a day. If a line describes exactly how you feel, that's the one to sit with.

Common questions

What Bible verse is for when you feel like giving up?

Galatians 6:9 speaks directly to weariness in doing good. Isaiah 40:29-31 and 2 Corinthians 12:9 are about strength arriving in weakness.

Which Psalm mentions being overwhelmed?

Psalm 61:2 uses the word: "when my heart is overwhelmed." Psalm 55:22 and Psalm 62 cover similar ground.
