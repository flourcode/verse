Bible verses for peace

Peace in the Bible isn't mainly the absence of noise. The Hebrew word, shalom, means wholeness, things being as they should be. Most of these verses describe peace that exists while the trouble is still going on.

A verse to start with

For peace

John 14:27

Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you; not as the world gives, I give to you. Don’t let your heart be troubled, neither let it be fearful.

World English Bible (WEB)

Why this fits

Spoken by Jesus hours before his arrest, to disciples who are about to scatter. He 'leaves' peace like a bequest and specifies it's not the world's kind, which depends on circumstances improving. Then two instructions: don't let your heart be troubled, don't let it be afraid. The peace and the fear are both real, and he's addressing both.

7 verses for peace

  1. John 14:27

    Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you; not as the world gives, I give to you. Don’t let your heart be troubled, neither let it be fearful.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Gospel (Jesus’ words and life)

    Spoken by Jesus hours before his arrest, to disciples who are about to scatter. He 'leaves' peace like a bequest and specifies it's not the world's kind, which depends on circumstances improving. Then two instructions: don't let your heart be troubled, don't let it be afraid. The peace and the fear are both real, and he's addressing both.

  2. Philippians 4:6-7

    In nothing be anxious, but in everything, by prayer and petition with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Letter (epistle)

    The peace here 'surpasses all understanding,' which is Paul's way of saying it won't make sense on paper. It arrives after specific prayer with thanksgiving, and it guards, like a soldier posted at the door of your heart and mind. If peace feels like something you have to manufacture, this passage describes it as something that arrives.

  3. Isaiah 26:3

    You will keep whoever’s mind is steadfast in perfect peace, because he trusts in you.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Prophecy

    'Perfect peace' in Hebrew is literally 'shalom shalom,' peace doubled. It's promised to the person whose mind is steadfast, fixed, leaning on God. This is part of a song of trust sung in Judah. The condition isn't a calm life; it's a mind that keeps returning to the same place.

  4. John 16:33

    I have told you these things, that in me you may have peace. In the world you have trouble; but cheer up! I have overcome the world.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Gospel (Jesus’ words and life)

    Jesus states the problem plainly ('in the world you have trouble') and doesn't soften it. The peace is 'in me,' located in a person rather than a situation. And 'cheer up' or 'take heart' is a command with a reason: the trouble has already been overcome.

  5. Psalm 4:8

    In peace I will both lay myself down and sleep, for you, Yahweh alone, make me live in safety.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Psalm (poetry)

    A bedtime psalm. David lies down and sleeps because God alone makes him live in safety. The earlier verses show he's under pressure from people spreading lies about him. Peace here is the ability to stop being on guard and rest.

  6. Colossians 3:15

    And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to which also you were called in one body, and be thankful.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Letter (epistle)

    'Let the peace of God rule' uses a word for an umpire, the one who makes the call. Paul is writing about relationships in a church, so this peace isn't purely private; it's meant to settle disputes and be shared. The verse ends 'be thankful,' which shows up next to peace more than once in Paul's letters.

  7. Numbers 6:24-26

    ‘Yahweh bless you, and keep you. Yahweh make his face to shine on you, and be gracious to you. Yahweh lift up his face toward you, and give you peace.’

    World English Bible (WEB) · Torah

    The oldest blessing in the Bible, given to Aaron to speak over the people. It ends with peace. God's face 'shining' on someone and being 'lifted up' toward them are pictures of favor and attention. People still use these words at the end of services and in cards because they've never needed updating.

Common questions

What Bible verse talks about peace that passes understanding?

Philippians 4:7: "the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus." The verse before it explains how to get there.

What is the shortest Bible verse about peace?

Psalm 4:8 is one sentence, and Numbers 6:26 ("give you peace") is the end of a three-line blessing.
