# Outreach

How Better Verses finds readers without a personal social presence. The rule under all of it: send the Comfort Kit, not the website. People can evaluate, use, and recommend a free printable; nobody knows what to do with "check out my site."

The outreach URL is **betterverses.com/comfort-kit**. It has no signup, no email gate, no donation ask, and nothing for sale, and the page says so.

## Priority order

1. Search (the situation pages do this on their own; keep writing them)
2. Resource-page links from churches, hospices, chaplaincies, caregiving groups
3. The printables themselves being handed around
4. Direct email to the people who curate resource lists
5. Caregiving and ministry newsletters and publications
6. The Better Verses email list
7. Directories (faith.tools after the site is two months old; Church Software Directory; thisweek.church; Ministry Forum's resource hub; Ministry Tools "People Helping")
8. Social media, only if there is ever a reason

Skip: personal Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, family. Product Hunt last, if ever; one link from a real grief-resources page is worth more than a hundred startup listings.

## The email

Subject: **Free Scripture comfort resource you may find useful**

> Hi,
>
> I maintain a small free site called Better Verses that helps people find Scripture for difficult real-life moments and read the passages in context.
>
> I recently put together a free printable Comfort Kit for hospital visits, grief, funerals, caregivers, and those moments when someone simply doesn't know what to say.
>
> I'm a layperson, not a pastor or counselor, so I've tried to keep it modest: Scripture, brief context, and practical guidance, without trying to replace pastoral care.
>
> There's no account, email gate, donation request, or paid product.
>
> If you think it would genuinely help the people who use your resource page, you're welcome to include it: https://betterverses.com/comfort-kit
>
> Either way, thanks for maintaining the resources you already do.
>
> Better Verses
> hello@betterverses.com

Change one line by audience. For pastors: "free pastoral-care Scripture printables." For caregivers and hospice: "free Scripture printables for difficult days." Same kit.

## Where to send it

Not only churches. The kit is for anyone who sits with someone who is hurting: hospice volunteer coordinators, hospital chaplaincy offices, bereavement groups, elder-care ministries, funeral celebrants, military chaplains, caregiver support groups, Stephen Ministry coordinators. Search "[city] hospice volunteer resources" and "grief resources" pages at churches; the ones with an existing list of links are the ones to write to.

## Articles for other sites

Useful, not promotional. No testimony, no "why Christians need this." Pitch:

- 5 things not to say when someone is grieving, and what to do instead
- Why the right Bible verse isn't always the most famous one
- How to choose Scripture for someone in the hospital
- When someone asks you for a Bible verse and you don't know what to say
- Why context matters more when someone is hurting

Bio: "Better Verses is a free Scripture-finding project built to help people find passages for real-life moments and read them in context." If a publication requires a legal name and that's uncomfortable, skip it.

## Listening, not marketing

Grief communities (r/GriefSupport and the like) are where you learn what hurts and what helps. Read them. Never post links in them.
