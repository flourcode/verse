Contact

The quickest way to reach us is email. We read everything, though we can’t always reply.

hello@betterverses.com

Useful things to write in

  • A verse looks wrong. Tell us the page and the reference. If the text differs from the World English Bible or the note misreads the passage, we’ll fix it.
  • A topic is missing. Say what you were facing when you looked and didn’t find it. Real situations, described plainly, are how we decide what to add next.
  • A page didn’t work. Your phone and browser, and what happened, are enough for us to find the problem.

Things we can’t help with

We aren’t counselors, pastors, or doctors, and we can’t give personal advice by email. If you’re in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line in your country right now. If you’re looking for someone to talk to about faith, a local congregation is a better place than an inbox.

Privacy

Emails you send us are used only to respond to you and to fix the site. We don’t add you to a list. See the privacy page for the rest.
