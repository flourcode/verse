Today

John 14:27

Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you; not as the world gives, I give to you. Don’t let your heart be troubled, neither let it be fearful.

World English Bible (WEB)

Jesus says this the night before his arrest, to friends who are about to watch him die and then be hunted themselves. He knows exactly how frightening the next few days will be, and he gives them peace anyway, with a note that it isn’t the world’s kind. The world’s peace is the absence of trouble. His is something that holds while the trouble is happening.
