THIS WEEK'S VERSE
Lamentations 3:22-23
"It is because of Yahweh’s loving kindnesses that we are not consumed, because his compassion doesn’t fail. They are new every morning. Great is your faithfulness."
(World English Bible)

Lamentations was written in the ruins of Jerusalem after the Babylonians
burned it. Most of the book is grief without relief. Then, in the exact
middle, this: mercy that is new every morning. The writer isn’t saying
things are fine. He’s saying that even here, waking up is itself
evidence that God hasn’t finished with him. That’s a stronger claim than
optimism, because it was made with the city still smoking.

A question to carry: What would it look like to treat this morning as a fresh supply rather than a continuation of yesterday?

FOR SOMEONE YOU CARE ABOUT
Bible verses for difficult coworkers. A difficult coworker can test your patience long before it tests your
theology. These passages focus on restraint, wise speech, forgiveness,
and the only part of the situation you fully control: your own response.

Proverbs 15:1
"A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger."

'A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.'
This is a proverb about escalation and how to stop it. It doesn't say
the other person will become reasonable. It says your reply is a lever,
and you get to choose which way to pull it.

If you're writing it in a card or a text: "Thinking of you today. This came to mind and I wanted you to have it."

FOR THOSE WHO COMFORT OTHERS
Grief and loss. After a suicide: a bruised reed he will not break. Say nothing from the
pulpit about where the person is, in either direction. Read Psalm 88 at
the service so the family hears that Scripture has a place for what
happened.

Isaiah 42:3
"He won’t break a bruised reed. He won’t quench a dimly burning wick. He will faithfully bring justice."

IS THAT IN THE BIBLE?
"money is the root of all evil" — Close, but the verse says the love of money is a root of all kinds of
evil, not money itself. The difference matters.

1 Timothy 6:10
"For the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil. Some have been led astray from the faith in their greed, and have pierced themselves through with many sorrows."
