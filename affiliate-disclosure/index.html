Affiliate Disclosure

Running a website costs money, even a small one. This page explains how Better Verses may cover those costs and what that does and doesn’t affect.

Advertising

The site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Ads are placed in clearly marked spaces away from the verses and notes. We don’t choose the individual ads, and an ad appearing here isn’t an endorsement.

Affiliate links

Some links on the site may be affiliate links. That means if you click one and buy something (for example, a printed Bible or a book), we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

As of September 19, 2026, the site contains no affiliate links. If that changes, this page will be updated first, and affiliate links will be marked where they appear.

What never changes

  • Which verses appear on a page, and what the notes say, are never influenced by advertising or affiliate income.
  • We don’t accept payment to include a verse, a product, or a link on a page.
  • We only link to things we would recommend to a friend without being paid.

Questions

If anything on the site looks like a paid placement and isn’t marked as one, let us know.
