Bible verses for work stress

Work stress is usually a mix of too much to do and too much riding on it. The Bible takes work seriously, more seriously than most religions, and it also insists on rest. These verses hold both.

A verse to start with

For work stress

Matthew 11:28-29

Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart; and you will find rest for your souls.

World English Bible (WEB)

Why this fits

'All you who labor and are heavily burdened' is a direct address to the overworked. Jesus doesn't offer to remove the work. He offers rest 'for your souls' and a yoke that's easy, which in farming means one that fits. Stress often means carrying a yoke that doesn't.

7 verses for work stress

  1. Matthew 11:28-29

    Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart; and you will find rest for your souls.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Gospel (Jesus’ words and life)

    'All you who labor and are heavily burdened' is a direct address to the overworked. Jesus doesn't offer to remove the work. He offers rest 'for your souls' and a yoke that's easy, which in farming means one that fits. Stress often means carrying a yoke that doesn't.

  2. Colossians 3:23-24

    And whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord, and not for men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance; for you serve the Lord Christ.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Letter (epistle)

    'Work heartily, as for the Lord, and not for men.' This reframes who the work is for, which changes how much the boss's mood or the review cycle can dictate your peace. First written to servants, it's the Bible's clearest statement that ordinary work has dignity.

  3. Psalm 127:2

    It is vain for you to rise up early, to stay up late, eating the bread of toil, for he gives sleep to his loved ones.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Psalm (poetry)

    Rising early, staying late, eating 'the bread of toil' is called vain here, not because effort is bad but because anxious effort forgets who provides. 'He gives sleep to his loved ones' is the correction. If work has eaten your sleep, this psalm is about you.

  4. Psalm 90:17

    Let the favor of the Lord our God be on us. Establish the work of our hands for us. Yes, establish the work of our hands.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Psalm (poetry)

    'Establish the work of our hands' is a prayer that what you do would last and matter. Moses prays it after reflecting on how short life is. It's a good sentence to say on the way into a hard day: let this count.

  5. Galatians 6:9

    Let’s not be weary in doing good, for we will reap in due season, if we don’t give up.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Letter (epistle)

    'Let's not be weary in doing good.' Paul admits weariness is the risk and answers it with a harvest 'in due season,' which means the payoff has a timeline you don't control. For work that feels unrewarded right now, the promise is that it's not unseen.

  6. Philippians 4:6-7

    In nothing be anxious, but in everything, by prayer and petition with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Letter (epistle)

    Work stress tends to be a list of specific worries. Paul's instruction fits a list: take each one and make it a request, with thanks. The peace that follows 'guards' your thoughts, which is what a mind spinning on Sunday night needs.

  7. Proverbs 16:3

    Commit your deeds to Yahweh, and your plans shall succeed.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Wisdom literature

    'Commit your deeds to Yahweh, and your plans shall succeed.' The Hebrew for 'commit' means roll onto, as if you're rolling a weight off yourself. It's not a guarantee of every project working out. It's a picture of handing over the outcome so you can do the work without carrying the whole result.

Common questions

What Bible verse is good for a stressful day at work?

Matthew 11:28 is the most direct. Psalm 90:17 works as a one-line prayer before a hard day.

What does the Bible say about burnout?

The word isn't used, but Psalm 127:2 describes anxious overwork as vain and calls sleep a gift, and Jesus' invitation in Matthew 11:28-30 is addressed to the exhausted. Rest is commanded, not just permitted.
