Bible verses for difficult coworkers

A difficult coworker can test your patience long before it tests your theology. These passages focus on restraint, wise speech, forgiveness, and the only part of the situation you fully control: your own response.

A verse to start with

For difficult coworkers

Proverbs 15:1

A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.

World English Bible (WEB)

Why this fits

'A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.' This is a proverb about escalation and how to stop it. It doesn't say the other person will become reasonable. It says your reply is a lever, and you get to choose which way to pull it.

7 verses for difficult coworkers

  1. Proverbs 15:1

    A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Wisdom literature

    'A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.' This is a proverb about escalation and how to stop it. It doesn't say the other person will become reasonable. It says your reply is a lever, and you get to choose which way to pull it.

  2. Romans 12:17-18

    Repay no one evil for evil. Respect what is honorable in the sight of all men. If it is possible, as much as it is up to you, be at peace with all men.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Letter (epistle)

    'If it is possible, as much as it is up to you, be at peace with all men.' Paul builds in two qualifiers because he knows peace isn't always possible and isn't entirely yours to make. What is yours: not repaying evil for evil, and being honorable in a way everyone can see. That's a workplace ethic in one sentence.

  3. Proverbs 19:11

    The discretion of a man makes him slow to anger. It is his glory to overlook an offense.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Wisdom literature

    'It is his glory to overlook an offense.' Overlooking isn't the same as being a doormat; it's choosing which battles are worth having. With a coworker who gives you daily small reasons to be offended, this proverb is practical: most of them can be let go, and letting them go is a strength.

  4. James 1:19

    So, then, my beloved brothers, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, and slow to anger;

    World English Bible (WEB) · Letter (epistle)

    Swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to anger. In that order. With difficult people, the temptation is to reverse it. James writes this to a community with real conflicts, and the advice hasn't aged. The slowness is the skill.

  5. Colossians 3:13

    bearing with one another, and forgiving each other, if any man has a complaint against any; even as Christ forgave you, so you also do.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Letter (epistle)

    'Bearing with one another' is the phrase for tolerating ongoing friction. 'If any man has a complaint against any' assumes you will have one. The standard for forgiving is how Christ forgave you, which is a high bar, and Paul knew it.

  6. Colossians 3:23-24

    And whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord, and not for men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance; for you serve the Lord Christ.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Letter (epistle)

    'Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord, and not for men.' When a coworker makes the job feel pointless or unfair, this verse changes who you're working for. It's addressed to servants in the first century, people with far less control over their work than you have.

  7. Proverbs 16:32

    One who is slow to anger is better than the mighty; one who rules his spirit, than he who takes a city.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Wisdom literature

    Ruling your own spirit is ranked above conquering a city. In a tense office, keeping your temper feels like the smaller achievement. The proverb says it's the bigger one. The verse pairs 'slow to anger' with 'rules his spirit,' the same skill seen from outside and inside. Nobody sees the emails you didn't send or the comeback you didn't make. Proverbs says those unseen victories are the bigger ones.

How to use these verses

Pick one line, such as Proverbs 15:1, and use it as a rule for one week: every reply to that person gets the gentle version. Read Romans 12:14-21 in full once; it's the Bible's most complete guidance on dealing with people who treat you badly.

Common questions

What does the Bible say about dealing with difficult people at work?

The most direct guidance is Romans 12:17-21: don't repay evil with evil, do what's honorable in everyone's sight, and be at peace as far as it depends on you. Proverbs adds a lot about controlling your reply (15:1, 19:11).

Is there a Bible verse about patience at work?

James 1:19 (slow to speak, slow to anger) and Proverbs 16:32 both apply directly. Colossians 3:23 is about doing your work well regardless of the people around you.
