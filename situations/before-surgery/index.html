Bible verses before surgery

The hours before surgery are mostly waiting, and waiting is when fear gets loud. These verses are short on purpose. They're about presence and peace, which is what you can hold onto when everything else is in other people's hands.

Nothing here is medical advice. Ask your care team every question you have; that's part of preparing well.

A verse to start with

For before surgery

Isaiah 41:10

Don’t you be afraid, for I am with you. Don’t be dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you. Yes, I will help you. Yes, I will uphold you with the right hand of my righteousness.

World English Bible (WEB)

Why this fits

'Don't be afraid, for I am with you.' Five promises in one verse, ending with being held up by God's right hand. It's addressed to people with real reasons to fear. It's the verse most often written on a card and slipped into a hospital bag.

7 verses for before surgery

  1. Isaiah 41:10

    Don’t you be afraid, for I am with you. Don’t be dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you. Yes, I will help you. Yes, I will uphold you with the right hand of my righteousness.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Prophecy

    'Don't be afraid, for I am with you.' Five promises in one verse, ending with being held up by God's right hand. It's addressed to people with real reasons to fear. It's the verse most often written on a card and slipped into a hospital bag.

  2. Psalm 23:4

    Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me. Your rod and your staff, they comfort me.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Psalm (poetry)

    'Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me.' The psalm doesn't say the valley is avoided. It says the walk through it isn't alone. Before surgery, that's the honest version of comfort.

  3. Philippians 4:6-7

    In nothing be anxious, but in everything, by prayer and petition with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Letter (epistle)

    Pre-surgery anxiety comes with specific questions. Paul's instruction is to make each one a request, then let peace stand guard. The peace 'surpasses understanding,' meaning it can be present even when the outcome is unknown. Paul's instruction has a structure that helps in a pre-op bed: take each specific fear, turn it into a specific request, and add one thing you're thankful for. The peace he describes stands guard over your thoughts; it doesn't require the outcome to be known.

  4. Psalm 46:1

    God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Psalm (poetry)

    'A very present help in trouble.' Present means now, not later. This is the verse for the pre-op room, when help needs to be immediate and you can't do anything but lie still. The psalm goes on to picture the earth changing and mountains shaking, then says 'Be still, and know that I am God.' Surgery involves handing control to other people. This psalm was written for people who had no control either.

  5. Exodus 33:14

    He said, “My presence will go with you, and I will give you rest.”

    World English Bible (WEB) · Narrative

    'My presence will go with you, and I will give you rest.' Moses asked for exactly this and got it. Going into an operating room, you go under, and rest is literally what happens. This verse promises the presence goes in with you.

  6. Psalm 4:8

    In peace I will both lay myself down and sleep, for you, Yahweh alone, make me live in safety.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Psalm (poetry)

    'In peace I will both lay myself down and sleep, for you, Yahweh alone, make me live in safety.' Written for a normal bedtime, it fits the night before surgery unusually well: lying down, sleeping, being kept safe by someone who's awake.

  7. Psalm 73:26

    My flesh and my heart fails, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever.

    World English Bible (WEB) · Psalm (poetry)

    'My flesh and my heart fails, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever.' The verse admits the body fails. That admission is what makes the second half believable. It's for someone whose body is the reason they're in this room.

How to use these verses

Write one verse on a card and take it with you; Isaiah 41:10 or Psalm 23:4 are the usual choices. If you're the one waiting in the family room, Psalm 46:1 is short enough to say over and over.

Common questions

What Bible verse should I read before surgery?

Isaiah 41:10 and Psalm 23:4 are the most common. Exodus 33:14 ("my presence will go with you") is a good one for the moment you're wheeled in.

What's a good verse to send someone having surgery?

Psalm 46:1 is one sentence and fits a text message. Isaiah 41:10 fits a card.
